Reconnect PFM Reporting Extract

The connection lapses if it's disconnected in QuickBooks Online, if it goes unused for about 100 days, or when Intuit's five-year limit on connections is reached.

To reconnect

  1. Contact the app's operator at report@pfmfreight.com.
  2. The operator starts the connection from the computer that runs the app.
  3. A QuickBooks Online administrator at PFM signs in to Intuit, chooses PFM's company and clicks Connect.

Operators: run python -m qbo_extract auth in the project folder, then python -m qbo_extract test to confirm.
