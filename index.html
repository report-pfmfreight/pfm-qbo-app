PFM Reporting Extract

A private, read-only connection that copies Performance Freight Management's own QuickBooks Online data into PFM's internal management reports.

The app connects to one QuickBooks Online company, PFM's. A QuickBooks administrator at PFM authorizes it, it runs on a schedule, and it has no public sign-up or user accounts. It only reads data. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks.
